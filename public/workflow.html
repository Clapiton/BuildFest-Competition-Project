🤖 AI Complaint Workflow

BuildFest 2026 — Track 5, Case Study 1  ·  Participant ID: BF-1079

1
🌐
Customer Submits Complaint
HTML intake form → POST /api/complaints · Input validation (400 on empty)
2
🧠
AI Classification
OpenAI gpt-4o-mini → category · urgency · sentiment · summary · draft reply  (Zod-validated, fallback on error)
3
🔀
Route to Team Member
Billing → Alice  ·  Product → Bob  ·  Service → Carol  ·  Other → Dave
4
📧
Send Notifications
Customer acknowledgment & team member alert via Resend · SMTP fallback · Console fallback
5
🗄️
Track Status in Database
received → in_progress → escalated → resolved  (Supabase Postgres + RLS)
6
⏱️
Schedule Escalation Timer
BullMQ delayed job added to Upstash Redis · 3 min (demo) / 24h (production)
7
🚨
Auto-Escalate if Unresolved
Reassign to manager@support.team · Status → escalated · Alert email sent · Audit event logged
or resolved before timer fires
✅
Manual Resolve
POST /api/complaints/:id/resolve → Status: resolved · Escalation job cancelled from Redis

📋 Audit Trail
